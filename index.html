Privacy Policy for BigButton Dial™

Effective Date: June 9, 2026

Welcome to BigButton Dial™ ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users, particularly seniors and individuals requiring accessibility features. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By downloading and using BigButton Dial™, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our core philosophy is simple: Your data belongs to you. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information to external servers.

Personal Data

  • No Server Storage: We do not collect personal identification information such as your name, phone number, email address, or address book contacts.
  • On-Device Only: Any configuration data, favorite contacts, or app settings you choose to set up inside BigButton Dial™ remain stored strictly on your local device.

Location Data

To provide the weather forecasting features on your dashboard, the App requires access to your device's location.

  • Purpose: We use your location coordinates (Latitude and Longitude) strictly to fetch real-time weather updates for your area.
  • Processing: This data is processed dynamically and sent directly to our third-party weather data provider (Open-Meteo).
  • Storage: We do not track your location in the background, nor do we store or share your historical location history.

2. Third-Party Services and SDKs

To provide specific functionalities and support app sustainability through advertisements, BigButton Dial™ utilizes trusted third-party software development kits (SDKs). These external providers may collect information used to identify your device.

You can review the privacy policies of the third-party providers we use below:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core app functionality, licensing, and stability. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may utilize your device's Advertising ID to serve relevant ads. Google Advertising Policies
  • Open-Meteo: Used to fetch current weather conditions and forecasts based on your location coordinates. Open-Meteo Privacy Policy

3. Device Permissions Required

To function effectively as an accessibility dialer and launcher, the App may request the following system permissions:

  • Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Required to retrieve local weather data.
  • Telephony / Phone (CALL_PHONE): Required to allow the user to initiate direct phone calls via the large button interface.
  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): Optional, used strictly to display your contacts on your screen so you can assign them to quick-dial buttons. This data never leaves your device.

4. Log Data and Error Reporting

In the event of an error or crash inside the App, code logs and diagnostic data may be automatically collected via Google Play Console reporting tools. This "Log Data" may include information such as your device Internet Protocol (“IP”) address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing the service, and the time and date of your use. This data is entirely anonymized and is used solely to fix bugs and improve app performance.

5. Children's Privacy

BigButton Dial™ is explicitly designed for seniors and general accessibility purposes. It does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our local configurations.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal compliance. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the effective date at the top.
