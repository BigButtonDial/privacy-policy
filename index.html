Privacy Policy for BigButton Dial™

Effective Date: July 12, 2026

Welcome to BigButton Dial™ ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users, particularly seniors and individuals requiring accessibility features. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By downloading and using BigButton Dial™, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our core philosophy is simple: Your personal identity belongs to you. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personally identifiable information to external servers. However, to monitor app stability and improve user experience, we collect anonymized, aggregated usage statistics as detailed below.

Personal Data

  • No PII Storage: We do not collect personal identification information such as your name, phone number, email address, or address book contacts.
  • On-Device Only: Any configuration data or app settings you choose to set up inside BigButton Dial™ remain stored strictly on your local device.

Location Data

To provide the weather forecasting features on your dashboard, the App requires access to your device's approximate location.

  • Purpose: We use your approximate location strictly to fetch real-time weather updates for your general area.
  • Processing: This data is processed dynamically and sent directly to our third-party weather data provider (Open-Meteo).
  • Storage: We do not track your location in the background, nor do we store or share your historical location history.

2. Third-Party Services and SDKs

To provide specific functionalities, trace app performance, and support app sustainability through advertisements, BigButton Dial™ utilizes trusted third-party software development kits (SDKs). These external providers may collect information used to identify your device.

You can review the privacy policies of the third-party providers we use below:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core app functionality, licensing, and stability. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (Firebase Analytics): Used to collect completely anonymized usage behaviors (such as button interactions, screen views, and retention statistics) to help us understand how the app is used and improve its interface. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may utilize your device's Advertising ID to serve relevant ads. Google Advertising Policies
  • Open-Meteo: Used to fetch current weather conditions and forecasts based on approximate location coordinates. Open-Meteo Privacy Policy

3. Device Permissions Required

To function effectively as an accessibility dialer and launcher, the App may request the following system permissions:

  • Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Required to retrieve local weather data. This grants the app an approximate idea of your location (such as city or neighborhood) to populate the weather widget without tracking your precise pinpoint coordinates.
  • Telephony / Phone (CALL_PHONE): Required to allow the user to initiate direct phone calls via the large button interface.

4. Log Data, Analytics, and Error Reporting

To ensure high performance and refine our accessibility interface, the App automatically logs basic diagnostic and analytics data via Google Play Console and Firebase Analytics. This data may include your device's anonymized mobile tracking identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), device name, operating system version, internal app event counts (e.g., taps on specific buttons), and the time and date of your use. This information is entirely pseudonymized and aggregated; it cannot be used to identify you personally and is utilized solely to fix bugs and enhance usability.

5. Children's Privacy

BigButton Dial™ is explicitly designed for seniors and general accessibility purposes. It does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our local configurations.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal compliance. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the effective date at the top.

7. Data Control & Deletion

Because BigButton Dial™ does not use user accounts or store personal data on external servers, there is no user data for us to delete.

For data automatically handled by third-party services (like Google AdMob or Firebase Analytics), users can control or delete their advertising data directly through their Android device settings by navigating to Settings > Privacy > Ads > Delete advertising ID.
